\textbf{Solution.}

Forward pass:
\[
h_1 = \sigma(x_1) = \frac{1}{1+e^{-x_1}}, \quad
h_2 = \sigma(x_2) = \frac{1}{1+e^{-x_2}}
\]
\[
o_{\min} = \min(h_1, h_2), \quad o_{\max} = \max(h_1, h_2)
\]
\[
y = o_{\min} + o_{\max}
\]

Backpropagation:
\[
\frac{\partial E}{\partial x_i} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial y} \cdot \frac{\partial y}{\partial h_i} \cdot \frac{\partial h_i}{\partial x_i}, \quad i=1,2
\]

Sigmoid derivative:
\[
\frac{\partial h_i}{\partial x_i} = \sigma(x_i)(1 - \sigma(x_i))
\]

Since \(\min(h_1,h_2) + \max(h_1,h_2) = h_1 + h_2\), we have \(y = h_1 + h_2\), thus:
\[
\frac{\partial y}{\partial h_1} = 1, \quad \frac{\partial y}{\partial h_2} = 1
\]

Therefore:
\[
\frac{\partial E}{\partial x_1} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial y} \cdot \sigma(x_1)(1 - \sigma(x_1))
\]
\[
\frac{\partial E}{\partial x_2} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial y} \cdot \sigma(x_2)(1 - \sigma(x_2))
\]

\textbf{Answer:}
\[
\boxed{\frac{\partial E}{\partial x_1} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial y} \cdot \sigma(x_1)\bigl(1 - \sigma(x_1)\bigr)}, \qquad
\boxed{\frac{\partial E}{\partial x_2} = \frac{\partial E}{\partial y} \cdot \sigma(x_2)\bigl(1 - \sigma(x_2)\bigr)}
\]
